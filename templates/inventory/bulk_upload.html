How packaging, modes and validation work

Packaging: for tablets bought in boxes of 100 - Purchase Unit = BOX, Units per Purchase Unit = 100, Purchase Quantity = 5, Purchase Price = 4200 (price of one box). The system stores 500 tablets at TSh 42 each.

Opening Stock: every upload creates new batches; it never adds to an old batch, even if the batch number is the same.

Restock / Purchase: tops up a batch only when product, batch number, expiry, cost, price and packaging all match exactly; otherwise a new batch is created. Rows must match an existing product (SKU required).

Automatic cleaning: extra spaces are removed, SKU is upper-cased, categories match existing ones regardless of case, units like "tablets" or "btl" are understood, prices like "TSh 4,200" are read correctly, and dates such as 31/01/2027 are converted.

Checks: required fields, whole-number quantities, negative or absurd values, past expiry dates, duplicate rows, SKU/barcode clashes (in the file and in your catalog), barcodes damaged by Excel, selling below cost, near-duplicate product names and unit conflicts with existing stock.

Expiry date is optional only for products that do not expire (requires_expiry = NO).
