Frequently asked questions

What happens if I buy a package I already have?

It is a top-up. The new SMS credits are added to your remaining balance. SMS credits never expire, so you never lose unused credits.

How long does activation take?

{% if online_payment_ready %}Online payments activate automatically as soon as they are confirmed. {% endif %}Manual mobile-money payments activate after the platform owner verifies your transaction reference.

Do the SMS services share credits?

No. Debt, Receipt, Marketing and Intelligent Report SMS each have their own credits, so heavy use of one never drains another. None of them expire.

I paid but my package is not active yet.

Check the status in Payment history below. Manual payments show “Pending” until verified. If it stays pending, contact support on {{ payment_settings.support_phone }} with your transaction reference.
