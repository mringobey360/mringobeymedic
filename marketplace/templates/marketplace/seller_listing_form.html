Listing checklist

  • Clear headline
    Type, size and area, e.g. “3-bedroom house, Mtumba”.
  • Honest price
    State the period and whether it is negotiable.
  • Exact location
    Region, district, ward and a GPS pin.
  • Bright photos
    Add several; the first one is the cover.
  • Reachable contact
    A phone that is answered, plus WhatsApp.

Customers enquire directly

Buyers and tenants call, WhatsApp or request a visit. There is no cart or stock for property listings.
