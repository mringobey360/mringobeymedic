# Intelligent Report SMS

Premium Daily Sales Intelligence is now a separate add-on service.

Official packages seeded by `python manage.py seed_daily_report_packages`:

| Package | Allowance | Price | Validity |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Intelligent Reports — 30 SMS | 30 report SMS | TZS 5,000 | 30 days |
| Intelligent Reports — 100 SMS | 100 report SMS | TZS 12,000 | 30 days |
| Intelligent Reports — 300 SMS | 300 report SMS | TZS 30,000 | 30 days |

Professional and Enterprise customers keep the existing included Intelligent Reports entitlement. The separate package is available to all pharmacy owners who need dedicated report-SMS delivery and a predictable allowance.

Each successful daily report SMS consumes one dedicated report-SMS credit. It no longer consumes the customer's standard SMS wallet.
