Meal payments & expected cash

Expected cash is what should physically be in the drawer: it already deducts approved expenses paid from the branch drawer. Earned meals are now deducted automatically as a meal payment when the employee scans (or the manager approves a network-outage claim) and sales fund it. Do not also record them as a Staff Meals expense. Pending meals, earned entitlements and meal debt are not automatically treated as cash paid. This prevents the system from deducting unpaid meals or counting a payment twice.
